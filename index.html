What the audit does

Backtest Reality Check is an independent validation lab for retail algo strategies (TradingView/Pine, Python, IBKR). We reproduce your backtest from your stated rules, then stress it with the same checks institutional quants use:

  • Deflated Sharpe ratio — corrects for how many variants you tried, so a winner picked from dozens of attempts is not mistaken for an edge.
  • Trade-concentration analysis — is one lucky trade carrying the whole result?
  • Risk-mechanics review — your stops, caps, and position sizing, checked against the rules you stated.

You get a written report with a PASS / FLAGGED / FAIL verdict on each section and the number behind every verdict. If your strategy is fragile, the report tells you exactly where — before the market does.

What you send us

Your strategy rules or code, the instruments, and the period you tested. We never need — and will never ask for — your brokerage login or account access.

How honest the report is — a real sample

Our sample audit excerpt covers a channel-breakout strategy tested over 2 years on 10 US and 10 Canadian tickers. The profit factors looked strong in isolation — and both cells were still verdict FLAGGED:

Why: across 53 evaluated variants, the raw Sharpe was about 0.17, and after deflating for the number of trials the deflated Sharpe ratio was 0.0 against a 0.95 required threshold — statistically indistinguishable from the best of many random trials. A strategy can be simultaneously “+29.7% in backtest” and “no statistical evidence of edge.” Both statements are in the report because both are true. Common questions

Is this financial advice?
No. It is a statistical audit of a backtest you provide — educational research. We do not recommend buying, selling, or trading anything.

What if my strategy fails the audit?
Then the audit did its job. You get the exact weakness (overfitting, concentration, unrealistic fills) in writing, plus what evidence would change the verdict.

Which platforms?
Pine Script / TradingView exports, Python strategies, or rules written in plain English. IBKR-based hobbyist setups included.

Can you guarantee my strategy will be profitable live?
No — and anyone who does is selling you something else. Backtests describe the past under assumptions; the report states those assumptions explicitly.

Do you optimize or tune my strategy?
No. The Premium tier tests neighbouring parameter values for fragility. Changing parameters until a test passes is exactly the overfitting this service exists to catch.
